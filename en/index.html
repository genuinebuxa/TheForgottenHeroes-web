About the game

The Forgotten Heroes is a turn-based fantasy strategy game. Every campaign is played on two stages: the adventure map, where heroes explore, gather and conquer, and the battlefield, where armies clash.

Exploration

The map reveals itself step by step. Your heroes pick up resource piles, capture mines and spend their movement points day by day.

Towns and armies

Every town belongs to a faction with its own creatures and dwellings. Seven levels, each with a basic and an upgraded creature.

Tactical battles

A hex-grid battlefield, turn order by initiative, shooters, flyers, large creatures and special abilities.

Factions

Three peoples, three ways to play. Each faction has 14 creatures: seven levels, a basic creature and its upgrade on each.

Necropolis

The realm of the undead

Dark slate walls, bone-white spired towers and a sickly green glow in the windows, under a violet sky and a pale moon.

Its army is made of skeletons, wraiths and death knights. Its creatures are undead: they know no fear and harvest souls.

Top row: levels 1–7 · bottom row: upgrades

Kingdom

The kingdom of men

Pale limestone walls, blue slate-roofed towers with golden finials, warm light behind the raised portcullis, a clear dawn sky.

Strong defence and shooters, swift cavalry and flying creatures at the top: spearmen, archers, knights, clerics and sky riders.

Top row: levels 1–7 · bottom row: upgrades

Horde

Orcs, goblins, trolls and mammoths

A sharpened log palisade hung with bones and hides, timber watchtowers, red firelight and a tusked skull above the gate, under a dusty orange-red dusk.

High attack, weak defence: many cheap low-level creatures, fast wolf riders and regenerating trolls.

Gameplay

Screenshots from the game in development. The art is still being made: several battle models are temporary placeholders.

Adventure map

A square-grid world of varied terrain that your heroes explore day by day.

  • Movement points, path planning, fog over unexplored tiles
  • Resources, mines, daily income and a calendar of weeks and months
  • Guarded mines and towns: win the battle before you take them
  • Map editor for your own maps

Battle

Two armies on a hex-grid 3D battlefield. Units act in order of initiative; whoever destroys every enemy unit wins.

  • Terrain obstacles, shooters, flyers and large creatures that fill two hexes
  • Retaliation, damage preview, wait and retreat
  • Abilities such as Fear Immunity, Life drain, Strike and return and many more

Play with friends

Against friends on one computer or over a local network.

  • Hot-seat: players take turns on one computer
  • LAN play: everyone commands their own heroes and battles on their own computer
  • Save, load and autosave at the end of every turn
  • Hungarian and English languages
